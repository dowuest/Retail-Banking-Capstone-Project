Scenario-Robust Conclusion

The SMB + Affluent investment thesis holds in all three scenarios. It is the only strategy that improves relative position in Base Case, limits damage in High Competition, and provides survival runway in Disintermediation. Generic mass-retail positioning is indefensible in any scenario. Every quarter of strategic delay is a quarter of relationship inception loss that cannot be recovered — the competitive clock runs independently of the rate cycle.
