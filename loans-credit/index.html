Undisputed profit engine

Mortgage
Origination barely viable
$443
Avg profit per loan (2024)
  • $12.61T outstanding; non-banks at 53.3% origination share

  • Servicing — not origination — is the value driver

  • CECL accounting punishes bank growth in unsecured portfolios
