Payments Ecosystem
Real-Time Payments
The defining structural shift
60–80%
Annual CAGR for RTP
  • FedNow (Jul 2023) now at 900+ institutions; community banks 78% of participants
  • FedNow per-transaction fee: $0.045 vs $15–50 for a domestic wire
  • $80T+ ACH volume is the migration prize
  • Real-time becoming table stakes for B2B and consumer disbursements
Embedded Finance & BaaS
Disaggregating the value chain
>40%
Annual growth in embedded payment volume
  • BaaS players: Unit, Synctera, Treasury Prime, Column
  • Software platforms (Shopify, Toast, ServiceTitan) now own acquiring economics
  • 750+ PayFacs in the US; ISVs taking meaningful share of acquiring
  • Highest deposit volume, zero relationship depth, margin compression
Digital Wallets & BNPL
Big Tech encroachment
3–5%
BNPL share of e-commerce GMV
  • Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal dominate mobile wallet share
  • BNPL charge-off rate declined to 1.83% in 2023
  • Wallets tokenize card credentials → fraud losses drop, but bank brand recedes
  • Apple, Google, Amazon building payments moats at massive scale
📉
Acquiring Margin Race to Zero
50–75bps → sub-20bps
<20bps
Enterprise acquiring spread (was 50–75bps)
  • Adyen eliminated 3rd-party acquirer markup entirely via direct acquiring licenses
  • Stripe: commoditize the transaction, monetize the intelligence (Radar, Capital, Atlas)
  • ISO model in structural decline; pure-play gateways functionally commoditized
  • Opportunity Software attachment (Clover, Toast, Shopify) is the only durable model
🔗
Cross-Border & Stablecoin
FX spread collapse
$1T+
Stablecoin outstanding (2025 projection)
  • Wise, Airwallex eliminated information asymmetry on bank FX spreads in major corridors
  • OCC granted national trust charters to Circle, Paxos, 3 others (Dec 2025)
  • Stablecoins eliminate FX conversion requirement for USD-equivalent flows entirely
  • Risk Correspondent banking shrinks to genuinely complex multi-currency institutional flows
